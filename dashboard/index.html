How the numbers are defined
  • Roadmap counts and SP figures come from the Notion “(Beta) TW Creative Roadmap” database, refreshed nightly by the sync routine.
  • Spend, impressions, clicks, installs and trial starts come from the BigQuery Meta funnel table (Taiwan + Hong Kong), which lags about two days; “last 7 days” means the last 7 dates in that table.
  • CPFT = spend ÷ trial starts. CPI = spend ÷ installs. CTR = clicks ÷ impressions. Win rate = rows with SP ≥ 2 ÷ rows with any SP score.
  • Days live = launch date to paused date (or to today while on air).
